   A) reporting

   C) reports
   D) which reports
   ANSWER: C  — The sentence has a subject ("The newspaper") but no main verb. "reports" is a complete main verb. An -ing form, a "to" infinitive, or a "which" clause cannot be the main verb of a sentence.

   A) The museum it

   ANSWER: C  — The verb "attracts" needs a subject that is just a noun phrase. "Because…" and "That…" turn the sentence into a dependent clause with no main clause, and "The museum it" repeats the subject.
