# Articles

English has two kinds of articles: **a/an** (indefinite) and **the** (definite). Sometimes no article is used.

**1. A or an?** The choice depends on the **sound**, not the spelling.
- **an** before a vowel sound: an hour, an honest mistake, an unusual property, an adult, an industrial laboratory.
- **a** before a consonant sound: a European, a university, a one-way street, a flightless bird.

**2. A singular countable noun always needs an article** (or another determiner such as "my" or "this"): "The kiwi is **a** flightless bird."

**3. Use "the"**:
- With **superlatives** and **ordinals**: **the** oldest, **the** deepest, **the** first person.
- When the noun is **specific** or limited by an "of" phrase: **the** summit of Mount Everest.
- With **unique** things: **the** Sun, **the** world, **the** Earth's surface.
- With names of **rivers, oceans, seas, mountain ranges, deserts**, and plural or "of" countries: **the** Amazon, **the** Pacific Ocean, **the** Rocky Mountains, **the** Sahara, **the** United States, **the** Philippines.

**4. Use no article**:
- With most **continents, countries, cities, single mountains, and lakes**: Africa, Canada, Mount Kilimanjaro, Lake Baikal.
- With **plural or uncountable nouns used in a general sense**: "**Giraffes** are the tallest land animals." "**Fish** survive under the ice."
- In fixed expressions: at sea level, by car, at night, most of.

## Examples

- Harvard is **the** oldest institution of higher learning in **the** United States.
- Light from the Sun takes more than **an** hour to reach Saturn.
- Magellan was **a** European navigator who died in **the** Philippines.

## Strategy

- Say the next word aloud in your head: vowel sound → "an"; consonant sound → "a".
- Before a superlative or "first/second/last", look for "the".
- With a place name, ask: river, ocean, range, or plural name (the)? Or continent, country, single mountain, or lake (no article)?

## TOEFL traps to watch for

- **"A" before a vowel sound**: "a adult", "a unusual", "a industrial".
- **"An" before a "y" sound**: "an European", "an university".
- **"A/an" with a superlative**: "a deepest point", "an oldest university".
- **Missing "the"** with rivers and mountain ranges: "Rocky Mountains stretch" should be "The Rocky Mountains".
- **Extra "the"** with continents and single mountains: "the Africa", "the Mount Everest".
- **Extra "the"** in fixed phrases: "at the sea level" should be "at sea level".
