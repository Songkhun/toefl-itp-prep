# Subjunctive

After certain verbs and expressions of **demand, recommendation, requirement, or importance**, the verb in the **that-clause** is in the **base form** (the subjunctive). It does not change for the subject or for past time.

**1. Verbs that take the subjunctive**: recommend, suggest, insist (meaning "demand"), require, demand, request, ask, urge, propose, advise.
- The doctor **recommended** that the patient **get** more rest. (not "gets")
- Officials **ordered** that all residents **leave** the area. (not "left")

**2. Adjectives that take the subjunctive**: It is **essential / necessary / important / vital / imperative / crucial** that ...
- It is **essential** that every passenger **wear** a seat belt. (not "wears")

**3. Form of the subjunctive**
- Base form for every subject: that he **submit**, that she **stay**, that it **be**.
- **Be** is used instead of am/is/are/was/were: that the report **be** completed.
- Negative: **not** + base form: that students **not** use phones during the test.
- If two verbs follow "that," **both** are base forms: that each hiker **carry** water and **follow** the trail.

**4. "Should" + base form** is also correct, especially in British English: "The committee recommended that the plan **should be** revised." TOEFL focuses on the American base form, but "should + base form" is not an error.

**Careful**: "insist" also means "say firmly that something is true." With that meaning, use a normal tense: "He insisted that he **was** innocent." "Suggest" works the same way when it means "show" or "indicate": "The evidence suggests that the climate **was** warmer."

## Examples

- The professor **required** that every student **submit** a research paper.
- The rules **require** that every entry **be** received by midnight.
- It is **important** that a scientist **keep** careful records.

## Strategy

- When you see recommend, insist, require, suggest, or "it is essential/important that," look at the verb after the subject in the that-clause.
- It should be the plain base form: no -s, no past tense, no "will."
- For "be," check that the sentence uses "be," not "is," "are," or "was."

## TOEFL traps to watch for

- **-s on the verb**: "required that every student submits" should be "submit."
- **Past tense after a past main verb**: "ordered that all residents left" should be "leave."
- **"Is" or "are" instead of "be"**: "require that every entry is received" should be "be received."
- **Mixed forms**: "required that each hiker carry water and follows the trail" should be "follow."
