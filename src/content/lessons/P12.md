# Inverted subject–verb order

In some structures, the **verb or auxiliary comes before the subject**, as in a question.

**1. Negative or limiting words at the beginning**: never, rarely, seldom, hardly, not until, not only, only + time or place expression, no sooner.

- **Rarely does a comet appear** bright enough to be seen in daylight.
- **Only in 1953 did scientists identify** the structure of DNA.

If there is no auxiliary, add **do/does/did**, and use the **base form** of the main verb after it. With "be", simply move "be": "Not only **was Amelia Earhart**..."

**2. "So/Neither/Nor" for agreement**: "Penguins cannot fly, and **neither can ostriches**."

**3. "So + adjective" at the beginning**: "**So thick is** the ice sheet that..."

**4. Conditionals without "if"**: **Had**, **Were**, or **Should** moves to the front: "**Were it not for** the atmosphere, ..."; "**Had** the canal not been built, ..."

**5. Place expressions at the beginning** with verbs like "lie, stand, sit, come": "Between the orbits of Mars and Jupiter **lies the asteroid belt**." Here the main verb itself moves; do **not** add "do".

Inversion happens **only when the negative word begins the sentence**. "Scientists rarely find..." uses normal order.

## Examples

- **Not until the 1960s did scientists find** strong evidence for continental drift.
- **Were the Moon** closer to Earth, ocean tides would be much stronger.
- Only during a total solar eclipse **does the corona become** visible to the naked eye.

## Strategy

- Check the first words of the sentence: is there a negative word, "only", "so", or a place phrase?
- If yes, look for auxiliary + subject + main verb.
- After "do/does/did", check for the base form.

## TOEFL traps to watch for

- **No inversion**: "Not until the 1960s scientists did find" should be "did scientists find".
- **Wrong verb form after "does/did"**: "does the corona becomes" should be "become".
- **"do" with "be"**: "Not only did Earhart the first woman" should be "was Earhart".
- **"do" after a place expression**: "does the asteroid belt lie" should be "lies the asteroid belt".
- **Missing "if" and missing inversion**: "The Moon were closer" should be "Were the Moon closer".
- **Extra pronoun**: "So thick it is the ice sheet that" should be "So thick is the ice sheet".
