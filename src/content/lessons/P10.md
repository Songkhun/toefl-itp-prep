# Parallel structure

When two or more items are joined by **and, or, but**, or are compared with **than** or **as**, the items must have the **same grammatical form**. This is called parallel structure.

- **Nouns with nouns**: "a painter, an engineer, and a student of anatomy"
- **Verbs with verbs** (same tense or form): "filter waste, regulate water balance, and help control blood pressure"
- **Adjectives with adjectives**: "testable, consistent, and able to predict"
- **Gerunds with gerunds, infinitives with infinitives**: "heating a new house ... than heating an old one"

In comparisons, compare like with like. "The climate of coastal cities is milder than **that of** inland cities" compares climate with climate. Use **that of** for a singular noun and **those of** for a plural noun.

## Examples

- The ancient Sumerians are known for **their writing system**, **their large cities**, and **their irrigation canals**.
- Large volcanic eruptions can **destroy** forests, **bury** towns, and **lower** global temperatures.
- Heating a well-insulated house costs less than **heating** a poorly insulated one.

## Strategy

- Find the joining word: "and, or, but, than, as".
- Identify the form of the first item in the series.
- Check that every other item matches it: noun, verb, adjective, -ing, or "to" + verb.
- In comparisons, ask: "Are the two things being compared really the same kind of thing?"

## TOEFL traps to watch for

- **-ing in a series of verbs**: "pollinate crops, produce honey, and making wax" should be "make".
- **A noun among adjectives**: "testable, consistency, and able" should be "consistent".
- **A clause at the end of a series**: "the blues, ragtime, and it borrowed from brass bands".
- **Mixed infinitive and gerund**: "costs less than to heat".
- **Illogical comparison**: "milder than inland cities" compares a climate with cities; use "than that of".
- **"that" vs "those"**: "the eyes of owls ... than that of other birds" should be "those".
