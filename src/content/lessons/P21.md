# Prepositions and fixed phrases

Many verbs, adjectives, and nouns are always followed by a particular preposition. These combinations must be learned as **fixed phrases**. TOEFL also tests prepositions of time and place.

**1. Verb + preposition**
- depend **on**, rely **on**, focus **on**
- consist **of**, approve **of**
- contribute **to**, belong **to**, lead **to**
- result **in** (the effect), result **from** (the cause), succeed **in**
- derive **from**, differ **from**

**2. Adjective + preposition**
- capable **of**, aware **of**, typical **of**
- responsible **for**, famous **for**, known **for** (the reason) / known **as** (a name or role)
- similar **to**, equal **to**, due **to**, prior **to**
- rich **in**, interested **in**

**3. Noun + preposition**
- an effect **on**, an influence **on**, an impact **on**
- the cause **of**, an example **of**, the eruption **of**
- a solution **to**, an increase **in**, a decrease **in**

**4. Multi-word prepositions**: in spite **of** (but "despite" with no "of"), instead **of**, because **of**, in addition **to**, according **to**, in search **of**.

**5. Time**
- **in** + year, month, century, season: in 1849, in the 1790s, in the eighteenth century
- **on** + day or date: on Monday, on July 4, 1776
- **at** + clock time: at noon, at 6:00
- **for** + a length of time (for 400 million years); **since** + a starting point (since 1900)

**6. Place and direction**: **from** ... **to** (from New Mexico to Canada), **into** (movement inside), **on** Earth.

## Examples

- Each molecule of water **consists of** two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom.
- The Internet has had a profound **effect on** the way people communicate.
- Sharks have existed **for** more than 400 million years.

## Strategy

- When a preposition is underlined, look at the word **just before** it. Is it part of a fixed phrase you know?
- For time expressions, ask: a year (in), a date (on), a clock time (at), a length (for), or a starting point (since)?
- Be careful: a few words accept more than one preposition. TOEFL tests clear errors, so learn the most common combination.

## TOEFL traps to watch for

- **Wrong preposition after a verb**: "rely in" should be "rely on"; "contributed for" should be "contributed to".
- **Wrong preposition after an adjective**: "capable to" should be "capable of"; "due on" should be "due to".
- **Extra "of"**: "despite of" should be "despite" or "in spite of".
- **"Since" with a length of time**: "since more than 400 million years" should be "for".
- **"On" with a year**: "On 1849" should be "In 1849".
