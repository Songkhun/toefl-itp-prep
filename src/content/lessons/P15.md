# Modals and verb forms after modals

**Modals** are can, could, may, might, must, shall, should, will, and would. They add meanings such as ability, possibility, necessity, and advice.

**1. Modal + base form.** The verb after a modal never takes -s, -ed, -ing, or "to".
- Some bamboo **can grow** nearly a meter in a day. (not "can grows", "can to grow")
- Concrete should be kept moist so that it **can reach** full strength.

**2. Modals themselves never change.** There is no "musts", "musting", or "must to". Even for past meaning, "could" is followed by the base form: "a letter **could take** weeks."

**3. Modal + be + past participle (passive)**: "Concrete **should be kept** moist."

**4. Modal + have + past participle** for the past:
- Possibility: "The dinosaurs **may have died** out after an asteroid impact."
- Guess: "Builders **might have used** ramps."
- Deduction: "The ground is wet; it **must have rained**."
- Criticism or regret: "They **should have checked** the data."

**5. Semi-modals**: have to, be able to, ought to, used to. These include "to" and are then followed by a base verb.
- People **used to preserve** meat with salt.
- Cacti **are able to survive** long droughts.

Do not confuse "**used to + base verb**" (a past habit) with "**be used to + -ing**" (be accustomed to).

## Examples

- Citizens **must be** at least eighteen years old to vote.
- Liquid water **may once have flowed** across the surface of Mars.
- A licensed pilot **must pass** a written examination and a flight test.

## Strategy

- Find the modal, then check the very next verb: it must be the base form.
- If the sentence is about the past, look for "modal + have + past participle".
- After "have" in that pattern, check for the past participle, not the base form.

## TOEFL traps to watch for

- **-s, -ed, or -ing after a modal**: "can reaches", "can stored", "might using".
- **"To" after a true modal**: "must to pass" should be "must pass".
- **"had" instead of "have"**: "might had existed" should be "might have existed".
- **Base form after "have"**: "might have exist" should be "might have existed".
- **"Be" verbs after modals**: "must are" should be "must be".
- **"Used to" + -ing**: "used to preserving" should be "used to preserve".
