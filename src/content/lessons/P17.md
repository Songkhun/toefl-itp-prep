# Singular/plural and countable nouns

**Countable nouns** have singular and plural forms (one planet, two planets). **Uncountable nouns** have no plural and do not take "a/an": information, evidence, equipment, research, advice, knowledge, furniture, machinery, homework, news.

**1. Quantity words**

| With plural countable nouns | With uncountable nouns |
|---|---|
| many, few, a few, a number of, several | much, little, a little, a great deal of, an amount of |

- **Few** written records survive. **Little** evidence survives.
- **A great deal of** equipment; **many** centuries; a different **amount** of fertilizer.

**2. "One of the" + plural noun**: "Jupiter is one of the **planets** that have rings."

**3. Each / every + singular noun**; "each of the" + plural noun: "Each of the **plants** received..."

**4. Numbers as adjectives stay singular**: "a **five-month** journey", "a **ten-year** study", "**several hundred** kinds". Use "hundreds of" or "thousands of" only without a number before them.

**5. Irregular plurals**: phenomenon → phenomena, criterion → criteria, species → species, analysis → analyses, nucleus → nuclei.

## Examples

- Scientists have gathered a great deal of **evidence** about the ancestors of whales.
- Relatively **few sailors** survived Magellan's voyage.
- The reef is home to more than a thousand **species** of fish.

## Strategy

- Check the noun after a quantity word: is it countable or uncountable?
- Check whether "information", "evidence", or "equipment" has been given an -s.
- After "one of the", "several", "many", and numbers above one, look for a plural noun.
- In a hyphenated number + noun before another noun, the noun stays singular.

## TOEFL traps to watch for

- **Plural uncountable nouns**: "evidences", "informations", "equipments", "researches".
- **"Much" or "little" with countable nouns**: "little written records" should be "few".
- **"Number" with uncountable nouns**: "a different number of fertilizer" should be "amount".
- **Plural number adjectives**: "a five-months journey" should be "a five-month journey".
- **"Several hundreds kinds"** should be "several hundred kinds".
- **Singular noun after "one of the"**: "one of the planet" should be "one of the planets".
