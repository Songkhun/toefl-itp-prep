# Reduced clauses and participle phrases

Long clauses are often shortened into **participle phrases**. An adjective clause such as "bats **that navigate** by echolocation" can become "bats **navigating** by echolocation". An adverb clause such as "**after he flew** the Atlantic" can become "**after flying** the Atlantic".

Choose the participle by asking who does the action:

- **-ing (present participle)** = active: the noun does the action. "Bats navigating by echolocation."
- **-ed / past participle** = passive: something is done to the noun. "The theory, accepted by most geologists, ..."

The same rule applies to feeling adjectives: **-ing** describes the cause ("amazing colors", "surprising results"), and **-ed** describes the person who feels ("surprised physicists").

An introductory participle phrase must describe the **subject of the main clause**. If it does not, the phrase "dangles": "Using a prism, white light was shown..." is wrong because white light did not use the prism.

## Examples

- **Completed** in 1931, the Empire State Building remained the world's tallest building for nearly four decades.
- The Ganges, **flowing** from the Himalayas to the Bay of Bengal, provides water for hundreds of millions of people.
- **Using** a glass prism, Isaac Newton showed that white light is a mixture of colors.

## Strategy

- Find the noun the phrase describes.
- Ask: does the noun do the action (-ing) or receive it (-ed)?
- For an opening phrase, check that the subject right after the comma is the one doing the action.

## TOEFL traps to watch for

- **-ing for a passive idea**: "Opening in 1869, the Suez Canal" should be "Opened".
- **Past participle of an intransitive verb**: "animals lived in the Cambrian" should be "living"; verbs like "live, flow, exist" have no passive.
- **Feeling adjectives reversed**: "physicists were surprising by the results" should be "surprised".
- **Wrong form after a preposition**: "after flown the Atlantic" should be "after flying".
- **Dangling participle**: the subject after the comma cannot logically do the action.
- **Extra main verb**: "Bats navigate by echolocation can detect insects" has two main verbs.
