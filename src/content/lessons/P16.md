# Pronouns

A pronoun must have the **correct form** for its job in the sentence and must **agree** with the noun it refers to (its antecedent).

**1. Subject and object forms**

| Subject | Object | Possessive adjective | Possessive pronoun | Reflexive |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | me | my | mine | myself |
| he / she / it | him / her / it | his / her / its | his / hers | himself / herself / itself |
| we | us | our | ours | ourselves |
| they | them | their | theirs | themselves |

- After a verb or a preposition, use the **object** form, even with "and": "The professor asked Maria and **me**." "between you and **me**".
- Before a verb, use the **subject** form: "**They** owned a bicycle shop."

**2. Possessive adjectives come before a noun**: "**their** skin", not "theirs skin".

**3. "Its" versus "it's"**: "its" is possessive ("the Moon has no light of **its** own"); "it's" means "it is" or "it has".

**4. Agreement in number**: a singular noun takes "it/its"; a plural noun takes "they/their".
- **A honeybee** moves **its** body. **Octopuses** change the color of **their** skin.

**5. Reflexive pronouns** refer back to the subject: "by **themselves**". "Theirselves" and "hisself" are not standard.

**6. Clear reference**: the pronoun must point to the right noun. If "the first person" refers to one woman, use "she", not "they".

## Examples

- Homing pigeons can usually find **their** way back to **their** lofts.
- Venus is similar to Earth in size, but **its** surface is far hotter.
- The results surprised the lead researcher and **me**.

## Strategy

- Find the noun the pronoun refers to. Is it singular or plural?
- Check the pronoun's job: subject, object, or possessive?
- Watch for long phrases between the noun and the pronoun.

## TOEFL traps to watch for

- **Singular/plural mismatch**: "pigeons can find its way" should be "their way".
- **"It's" for "its"**: "it's surface" should be "its surface".
- **Subject form after a verb**: "surprised the researcher and I" should be "and me".
- **Object form as a subject**: "a shop that them owned" should be "that they owned".
- **Plural pronoun for a singular animal**: "the giant panda ... their daily needs" should be "its".
